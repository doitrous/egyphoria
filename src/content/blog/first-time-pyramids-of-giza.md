Nothing quite prepares you for the scale. You've seen the Pyramids of Giza a thousand times in photographs — and then you stand at the base of one, look up, and every picture you've ever seen suddenly feels small.

Here's how to make your first visit count.

## When to go

Arrive early. The plateau opens in the morning, and the first hours bring softer light, gentler heat, and far fewer crowds. Late afternoon is the other golden window — the stone warms to amber and the shadows stretch long across the sand.

## What you'll see

- **The Great Pyramid of Khufu** — the oldest and largest, built more than 4,500 years ago.
- **The pyramids of Khafre and Menkaure** — Khafre's still wears a cap of its original casing stones.
- **The Great Sphinx** — smaller than most imagine, and all the more mysterious for it.
- **The panorama point** — a short drive from the main entrance, where all three pyramids line up for the photograph you came for.

## Small things that make a big difference

Bring water and sun protection — there's little shade on the plateau. Wear comfortable shoes for uneven ground. And leave time. The temptation is to rush from one monument to the next; the reward is in slowing down.
